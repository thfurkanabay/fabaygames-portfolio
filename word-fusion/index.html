Meaning, patterns and connections

Gameplay

Players select four related words to reveal a hidden category, then keep solving groups until the board is clear. The Master Word adds a broader connection between groups.

My design & development focus

I use Word Hints and Group Hints to support players when a connection is hard to spot. My focus is on short puzzle sessions built around vocabulary, reasoning and discovering relationships between words.
