Perfect Shot: Soccer

Aim, shoot and score in quick football challenges built on skill and accuracy.

Get it on Google Play ↗All published games ↗

A compact aiming-and-release loop

Gameplay

Pull the ball back, choose an angle and release to shoot. A visible trajectory helps the player plan a shot, while defenders and a goalkeeper turn accuracy into a short tactical challenge.

Design & development focus

Limited attempts give each shot weight. Quick restarts, league and cup progression, rewards and boosters support short mobile sessions, with a clean black-and-white visual direction.
