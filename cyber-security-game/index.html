RESEARCH / GAME DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT

CyberQuest.

A cybersecurity simulation I worked on to help small and medium enterprise managers explore secure technology adoption.

Play CyberQuest ↗

About the project

CyberQuest places SME managers in a virtual environment where they explore cybersecurity choices while adopting new digital technologies. The experience connects business decisions with practical security considerations.

Gameplay & scenarios

Players identify critical assets, assess the security implications of emerging technologies, evaluate technology vendors and consider how to integrate new tools securely. Scenario-based decisions help players practise balancing innovation with risk.

My role & contributions

I worked on CyberQuest as a Game Designer and Supporting Software Developer. I contributed to the game design and supported development across code, screens, selected gameplay mechanics, 3D models and the game environment.

  • Game design and interactive scenario design
  • Contributing code and building some of the game screens
  • Implementing selected gameplay mechanics
  • Creating 3D models and working on the game environment

Design focus

My work brings cybersecurity topics into interactive situations and decision points. Risk assessment, vendor evaluation and adaptive decision-making form the core of the learning experience.

Topics covered

  • The CIA triad: confidentiality, integrity and availability
  • Virus and malware protection
  • Access control
  • Vendor evaluation and secure technology adoption
