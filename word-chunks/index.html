Gameplay

Players combine chunks rather than individual letters to form complete words. Hidden, secret and bonus words add extra goals to the word-building loop.

My design & development focus

I built the experience around play without timer pressure, using city destinations, collections and coin rewards to give players a sense of progress at their own pace.
