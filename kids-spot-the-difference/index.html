Kids Spot the Difference

Spot fun differences! A brain game to boost kids' focus and observation.

Get it on Google Play ↗All published games ↗

Observation through picture puzzles

Gameplay

Two similar illustrations invite players to look closely and tap the differences. Familiar themes such as animals, toys, nature and food give the puzzles a friendly visual setting.

My design & development focus

I focused on a simple touch interface and helpful hints to support careful observation. Most levels allow play without time pressure, with an experience designed for both phones and tablets.

Behind the gameplay

In my current Unity development, I am working on Firebase player-flow analytics, rewarded ads, Unity IAP and restoration, persistent progression, localisation and guided tutorial interactions.

The published build and ongoing development can differ. My engineering notes explain the systems and their current scope.
